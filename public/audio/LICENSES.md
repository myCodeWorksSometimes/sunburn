on 18 October 2026. Each page identifies its release as **CC0** (public-domain
dedication): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Attribution is
provided for provenance even though CC0 does not require it. Files are bundled
locally; no streaming service or network connection is required while playing.

boss layer are offline edits of recorded environmental/machinery material.
No franchise music/voices are copied. Human headphone/speaker listening remains
necessary to judge the emotional fit and final mix.
