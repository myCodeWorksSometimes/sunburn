## What happens here (so the art fits)

Rowan crosses a rope bridge from the village campfire into the town playground,
where Eli was when the meteor struck. Lighting the playground fire makes an old
swing move again, and he rides it across a crater. In the treehouse he finds Eli's
drawing and ember cup, then takes the service path to the canal, where a
waterwheel's sluice lets him cross toward the clock tower.

The **clock tower** should be visible in the distance in all three, getting
closer from left to right.
