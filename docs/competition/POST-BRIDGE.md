# Post-bridge route and testing shortcuts

Camp crossing → playground swing → treehouse drawing/cup → canal sluice →
Eli's tower window / three knocks → Old Road → home / last hearth ember →
cellar passage / Old Road → three bells → Warden → Eli / transfer → view →
refuge → home / answer knocks. Mara remains a dead echo. Tomas/Nia live only
if Rowan personally escorts them across the secured bridge.

Type a code directly with the game focused. After `afaq`, gameplay pauses while
you finish. Escape or a 2.5-second gap cancels. Codes work from the title,
gameplay and open map/satchel. Test runs supply earlier prerequisites and leave
the normal save intact. `afaqback` restores its safe checkpoint. Reload returns
to the normal save rather than the test fixture.

| Code | Starting point / unfinished objective |
| --- | --- |
| `afaqbridge` | Main bridge west bank, brake set; restore and cross |
| `afaqisasleep` | Main bridge far bank / Act 2 approach; camp crossing |
| `afaqswings` | Playground fire; moving swing crossing |
| `afaqtree` | Treehouse fire; climb and collect drawing/cup |
| `afaqcanal` | Canal fire; climb mill and turn wheel |
| `afaqtower` | Tower courtyard; find Eli and fetch hearth ember |
| `afaqbells` | Courtyard with ember; three bells unfinished |
| `afaqwarden` | Tower crown, three bells rung; fight Warden |
| `afaqrescue` | Warden defeated; climb to Eli and transfer ember |
| `afaqending` | Home with Eli; sit together and answer his knocks |
| `afaqback` | Normal adventure at its safe checkpoint |

H requests contextual help immediately. After eight seconds without meaningful
progress, a hint and pulsing destination marker appear automatically. They identify
the next fire, ledge, wheel or door, a dropped lantern, or insufficient flame.
Dialogue and boss overlays retain their own safe space.

## Focused human checks

1. `afaqisasleep`: E at the nearby campfire, wait for the light to expand, then
   walk right across the painted rope bridge. Return after expiry: it stays solid.
2. At the playground fire, restore the swing and wait for its board to approach
   the near bank. D+Space onto it; ride right, then jump to the far bank. The
   memory lasts26s; the return deck appears only after crossing successfully.
3. `afaqtree` / `afaqcanal`: climb, collect the drawing/cup, and hold E at the
   wheel. H should point up to the next tread. Hold R at a fire for2s to refill.
4. Open M after dialogue finishes, during speech, with the lantern on the
   ground, on the return-home objective, and after rescue. Esc must resume.
5. `afaqbells`: three climbs; `afaqwarden`: combat balance; `afaqrescue`: Eli and
   gallery remain after expiry; `afaqending`: playable knock response. Then
   `afaqback` should recover your normal adventure.
6. Longer acceptance remains human: fresh new-game-to-credits, escort/retry timing,
   subjective artwork/lighting, boss difficulty and headphone mix. Report the
   code, location, action and screenshot when something fails.
